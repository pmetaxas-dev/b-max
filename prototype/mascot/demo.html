Lumi: small lamp, body emerging from it

The lamp (bulb ~45px) is Lumi's head and her permanent form: it never grows or disappears. "Emerge Lumi" brings the body out of the lamp: it starts folded up inside the lamp, comes down out of the collar (torso, then legs, then arms), and stays attached. "Return to lamp" folds it back in. Position lives on the anchor only: "Move anchor (test)" moves the anchor and the body follows. Demo-only aids: guides, X-ray (see the body inside the lamp) and zoom (the real size is small). No text, voice or dragging yet.
