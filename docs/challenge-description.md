Problem

Many people with ADHD struggle to stay focused, organize their day, manage time, and complete tasks. Small distractions can quickly derail attention, while constant mental overload and sensory stimuli increase stress and make it difficult to stay on track. Existing productivity tools often require more organization than the user can consistently maintain.
Challenge

How might we help people with ADHD stay focused, organized, and in control of their daily lives without adding complexity or becoming another distraction?
Example ideas

    AI personal coach or companion
    Context-aware reminders
    Smart task prioritization
    Focus and distraction management
    Sensory-friendly experiences
    Wearables that support attention and self-regulation
    Gentle guidance to return to interrupted tasks
